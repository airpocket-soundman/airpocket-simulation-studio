# Persistable facet-fixed complex pressure operator

[日本語](../curved-pressure-operator.md)

`src/curved-pressure-operator.js` adds a bounded, revisioned persistence contract for the existing `curved-pressure-transfer.js` face-constant complex pressure transfer and transpose. Existing APIs are unchanged. No solver/Worker/GUI/MCP is connected. Geometry consists of explicitly oriented triangular facets, excluding smooth-surface accuracy, nonmatching projection, P1 nodal pressure and iterative coupling. `solverConnected:false` and `smoothSurfaceAccuracy:false` remain explicit.

Geometry is `{lengthUnit:'mm',surface:'open'|'closed',nodes:number[],triangles:[i,j,k][]}`. The existing public validator checks finite coordinates, indices, degeneracy/duplicates, and closed-shell edge incidence/orientation consistency. Normals follow declared winding without automatic flipping or inference of physical outward direction. Self-intersection and general CAD conformity are not certified. All coordinates, including unused nodes, require numbers; geometry identity binds the full geometry.

`buildCurvedPressureTransferOperator(geometry)` stores each faceIndex, node IDs, normal, area in mm² and area/3 weight in m². Weight and normal remain separate factors to prevent eager multiplication from underflowing tiny normal components. Pressure is `{units:'Pa',values:[{real,imag},...]}` in original triangle order. `applyCurvedPressureTransferOperator(op,geometry,pressure)` returns complex nodal force, resultant N and origin moment Nm. Forces use `F=-p n A/3`; moments sum `r×F` after converting mm coordinates to metres.

Displacement is `{units:'m',real:number[],imag:number[]}` in node xyz order. `applyCurvedPressureTranspose` uses the identical weights to return `V=∫u·n dA` per face in m³. If force operator L=-B, then V=-Lᵀu: the force sign must not be copied into volume. `evaluateCurvedPressureVirtualWork` checks `Σconj(u)·F = -Σp·conj(V)` in J. Tolerance is1e-18J plus1e-12 times scaleJ, the sum of absolute product contributions on both sides. Despite its legacy spelling, relativeErrorJ is dimensionless absoluteErrorJ/scaleJ (absolute error when scale0), differing from the older API's denominator. Zero resultant does not erase nonzero nodal forces or work.

Each apply checks SHA-256 identities of snapshotted geometry/fields, returning pressureRevision or displacementRevision. Mutation during hashing cannot alter the accepted snapshot; applying to changed geometry later is refused. Outputs are detached, and deeply frozen operators/factors are issued through a WeakSet.

`serializeCurvedPressureTransferOperator(op)` accepts only issued operators. `loadCurvedPressureTransferOperator(raw,geometry)` checks raw capacity/depth and operatorRevision, then rederives all factors from geometry. Rehashed factor/normal/node/unit/scope modifications are still refused. It returns `{operator,source:{bytes,rawIdentity,meaning}}`; rawIdentity is the original UTF-8 JSON SHA-256, not authorship, authentication or accuracy evidence.

Limits are4096 nodes,512 faces, coordinate magnitude≤1e6mm,1MiB raw JSON,100000 graph values and depth12. Only plain records and ordinary dense arrays are accepted. Accessors, custom prototypes, unknown fields, nonfinite values and invalid indices are refused. Geometry/field array lengths are checked before allocation. Positive areas whose weights cannot be represented in double precision are refused rather than forced to zero, as are nonfinite intermediate products or sums.

[Evidence](../evidence/2026-10-24-curved-pressure-operator.json) and regressions include an independently supplied2×3mm triangle with p=2+3i Pa and uz=(1,2,4)+i(-1,3,2)mm: each nodal Fz=-(2+3i)e-6N, V=(7+4i)e-9m³ and W=-(26+13i)e-9J. A noncoplanar skew closed tetrahedron uses independent SI area vectors/centroid moments and complex rigid translations/rotations with origin shifts. Existing force/integral values, huge finite displacement, tiny-normal factor underflow, zero resultants, reload/rehashed tampering, simultaneous maximum capacities, exact1MiB/+1byte refusal and public types are verified. These are synthetic numerical references, not completion of parent COUPLE4B or general physical accuracy.
