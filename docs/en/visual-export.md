# Images and animations

Single-view transient-acoustic time WebM saving now uses fixed VP8 frames with full decoded-frame and source-endpoint verification. Preview and other WebM animations retain realtime behavior. [Scope, time ledger and limits](single-view-acoustic-fixed-webm.md).

API/MCP visual-export start, status and cancel summaries include read-only `executionSettled`. It starts `false` and becomes `true` only after renderer and artifact processing settle and release shared renderer exclusion. Cancellation changes `state:"cancelled"` first; that state alone does not establish cleanup completion. Poll `exportStatus({jobId})` until `executionSettled:true` before `releaseExport`. This describes visual-export execution separately from analysis jobs; a timeout alone never marks an unresolved renderer settled.

Disconnect cancels only exports started through that connection. The old session becomes unavailable. After explicit reconnection to the same page, a known retained jobId can be inspected through `exportStatus`; a cancelled artifact being unavailable is a state refusal, not a cross-session ownership refusal. GUI-started user exports are outside this connection-owned cancellation. Released job IDs remain unknown and cannot report settlement.

Time-comparison GIF manifests include `time.frameTimings`, binding each render request and panel's stored time/offset to case/result identity. See [details and bounds](view-compare-design.md). GIF records 24 output frames. The [fixed comparison-time WebM path](comparison-fixed-webm.md) records physical selection and presentation timing for every verified encoded/decoded frame. Realtime orbit/deformation WebM retains its policy summary without that guarantee. Overflow fails without returning a partial artifact.

`executionSettled` means the export Promise chain and queue exclusion have finished. It does not independently prove successful output or OS/codec-internal resource or memory release.

Open **Save images and animations** to save the current view as PNG or animation as WebM. Model-only PNG and orbit are supported. Images include model name, range/units and section information, with a maximum width of 1920 pixels.

Combine result surface, surface triangles, internal mesh and clipping. Exports use the current rendered view. Hide the surface to show internal mesh alone.

Four types: orbit, structural/modal deformation cycle, stored transient acoustic frames, moving section. Set 1–15 seconds and choose playback or WebM recording. Orbit/deformation/section recording requires MediaRecorder support and selects available VP9/VP8 codecs. Stored-time WebM instead requires VP8 VideoEncoder/VideoDecoder support. Completion/stopping restores camera, deformation scale, section and time.

Deformation cycles scale stored displacements without recalculating load histories or nonlinear trajectories. Modal shapes are normalized to 1 mm maximum displacement, not physical amplitudes. Transient playback labels physical snapshot times; video time is stretched to the chosen visualization duration. Frame rate depends on rendering performance. Single-view GIF, MP4 and streamline/particle animation are unsupported. Two-case GIF separately supports orbit/time/visualization scale.

The fixed-frame PNG-sequence (`png-sequence`) ZIP is byte-identical when the same input is solved again. `resultRevision` includes the wall-clock `result.performance.solveMs`, so it changes with every fresh solve (it is the value stored case, measurement and named-view records rely on, and it is unchanged). `getResultView` therefore also returns `physicalRevision`, the SHA-256 of the same output with `result.performance` removed. The in-ZIP `manifest.json` (`schemaVersion` 2) records `physicalRevision`, per manifest and per frame, instead of `resultRevision`. The artifact metadata `manifest` keeps `resultRevision` and adds `physicalRevision`. `schemaVersion` 1 ZIPs (recording `resultRevision`) remain readable.

For transient heat, API/MCP export jobs support a ZIP of stored temperature frames with `format:"png-sequence", animation:"time"`. The result needs at least two stored transient-thermal snapshots and the selected field must be temperature. `frames` is 2–120 and cannot exceed the stored count. Frames are selected by stored index without interpolation, so physical times need not be equally spaced. The manifest records each frame's `snapshotIndex` and `physicalTimeSeconds` and identifies the temperature time basis. Heat flux is excluded. Thermal real-time preview remains unsupported. Stored temperature WebM is supported separately through `singleThermalTimeWebmExport` and the GUI Stored time → Save WebM action, with heat-flux refusal; the general GUI animation controls do not export PNG sequences. Discover supported kinds through `exportCapabilities().formats["png-sequence"].timeAnimationResultKinds` and the thermal field through `thermalTimeField`. The top-level `timeAnimationResultKinds` continues to describe acoustic-only real-time playback. Localhost real-Chrome verification of this thermal PNG-sequence addition is recorded below.

2026-10-17 localhost real-Chrome evidence: `../evidence/2026-10-17-thermal-time-api-real-chrome.json`. A small tetrahedral result from actual `solveThermal` (T = 20 + t) verified API selection at 5/10/2 s (25/30/22 degC), omitted non-final flux and available final flux, invalid/stale guards, PNG-sequence times 0/5/10 s with validated hashes and restoration to 2 s, and the comparison panel at 5 s with restoration to 2 s. This proves the small localhost scenario; public deployment and arbitrary meshes were not checked. Interpolation restoration and generic patches have automated-test coverage only. Browser download remains unverified after a download-event timeout and debugger detachment.

2026-10-19 slice PNG-sequence reference: `../evidence/2026-10-19-opencode-browserexport1.json`. Against the volume-mesh axis range 0–10 mm of a real solve (10×10×40 mm box, E = 1 GPa, poisson 0.3, z=0 face fixed, 100 N in +Z on the z=40 face, mesh size 5 mm, elastic wasm-pcg), `format:"png-sequence", animation:"slice", frames:3` produced a ZIP with `slicePosition` 0/5/10 mm at phase 0/0.5/1. An independent STORE reader verified the manifest, the three CRC-32 values, the three SHA-256 values and the PNG IHDR (canvas value 320×316); the `clip-position` seen by the renderer matched the manifest `slicePosition`, and re-running with the same frozen view, locale and result was byte-identical. Cancelling restored the section position, section toggle, camera and controls, and `exportArtifact` refused the partial ZIP. This is a Node unit/helper check: the frame pixels come from a test-double PNG encoder, not a real canvas, and real-Chrome/MCP `startExport` → full artifact page retrieval → SHA comparison was not run. 120-frame success, the 4 MiB/24 MiB caps and general shapes remain unverified. `manifest.json` has no unit field for `slicePosition`; mm follows the geometry units (built-in shapes are mm).
