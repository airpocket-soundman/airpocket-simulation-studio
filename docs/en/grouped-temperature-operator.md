# Temperature P1 L2 transfer across explicit planar groups

[日本語](../grouped-temperature-operator.md) · [Single-plane temperature projection](planar-temperature-operator.md) · [P1 heat flux](planar-p1-heat-operator.md)

The pure API in `grouped-temperature-operator.js` transfers scalar nodal temperature on an explicitly supplied finite set of planar triangle groups. Each group has full matching coverage. Together they may form a folded interface or disjoint patches. No group inference, smooth curved surfaces, partial coverage, thermal-contact/coupled solver, GUI, measurement or calibration is added. `solverConnected:false` remains explicit.

Input is `{units:'mm',mesh:{nodes},sourceFaces,targetFaces,groups}`. A single flat xyz array supplies global node IDs. Each face is `{nodes:[globalId0,globalId1,globalId2]}`. Each group is `{groupId,sourceFaceIds,targetFaceIds}`, using zero-based indices into the corresponding complete face arrays. Every source and target face must occur exactly once: reject unknown, missing and duplicate assignments. Unique group IDs contain1–64 ASCII alphanumeric/underscore/hyphen characters. Group order and membership enumeration are canonicalized; global face order, winding and node IDs remain significant. Coincident coordinates never cause automatic welding.

Every group reuses unchanged single-plane coverage, planarity, opposing-normal, same-side overlap and numeric/capacity validation. Additional bounded triangle-pair checks reject same-side cross-group area overlap. Intersections between different planes may occur only on shared global vertex/edge IDs. Contact using distinct IDs or an interior crossing is refused. Numeric contact distance is1e-6mm; the coplanar normal-dot tolerance is1e-10; coplanar area floor is1e-12mm²+1e-12 times the smaller triangle area. Unstable triangle-intersection arithmetic is refused. This is a limited check of supplied triangle sets, not general CAD repair or smooth-surface intersection detection.

Recover group matrices from their area normalization and scatter-add by global IDs. For total area A, assemble `M=Σ A_g/A M_g` and `T=Σ A_g/A T_g`, then perform one global Cholesky solve for `θ_B=M^-1 T θ_A`. Never project separately and average shared target values. Each shared node has one scalar degree of freedom, including all incident mass terms. Temperature does not reverse sign with opposing normals. Local operators already convert mm² to m²; global assembly uses area ratios.

Budgets:1–8 groups;4096 total mesh nodes;512 total faces/side;128 global active nodes/side; at most16384 coefficients each in M/T/R;1MiB JSON;200000 graph values/depth20;1024-character generic strings. Existing local limits also apply. Global κ∞ must not exceed1e10, even when every local mass is well conditioned. Reject nonpositive pivots, nonfinite arithmetic, overflow and residual excess without regularization. Compile residual is1e-12×max(1,|Tij|); apply residual is1e-10×max(1,|θ|); integral tolerance is the latter scale times total area.

Fields have exactly one temperature per global active source ID: `{units:'degC'|'K',values:[{nodeId,value}]}`. Units are homogeneous across the field; no mixed rows/implicit conversion/unknown or duplicate nodes. Reject inputs below absolute zero or with magnitude above `Number.MAX_VALUE/(128*1e10)`. Consistent L2 does not preserve source min/max or positivity. At the projected absolute-zero boundary only1e-10 rounding error is permitted, without clamping. Returned degC*m2/K*m2 quantities are **area-temperature integrals**, not energy conservation without heat capacity, density, volume and time.

Build/apply/load snapshot plain records/dense arrays before their first await, without invoking accessors. Only privately issued operators may be applied or serialized. Loading rederives group assignments, local T/M/area, global matrices/factor/R/weights/conditioning and identities, then compares the complete canonical record. Self-rehashed tampering is refused. geometryRevision identifies the entire manifest, including unused nodes. rawIdentity is the input JSON UTF8 SHA, not authentication. Existing single-plane and flux module behavior remains unchanged.

[The synthetic oracle](../evidence/2026-10-26-grouped-temperature-operator.json) uses two .5mm² triangles sharing edgeAB across a fold. A center fan hat1 with zero boundary on one side, zero on the other, gives `M=(1e-6/24)[[4,2,1,1],[2,4,1,1],[1,1,2,0],[1,1,0,2]]`, `b=1e-6*[1/18,1/18,1/18,0]` and projected increment `[1/6,1/6,1/2,-1/6]`. Separately projecting and averaging instead gives C=1/3,D=0. A global3D affine20+10x+30y+40z reproduces target[20,30,50,60] and integral35e-6degC*m2. Scaling lengths by10 plus a rigid transform leaves temperatures unchanged and scales the integral by100. These fixed independent formulas require no real solver/GUI/codec. They do not complete the COUPLE3 parent task.
